JobsHunter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-02

This Privacy Policy explains how JobsHunter collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use JobsHunter services, including APIs, MCP integrations, and related web features.

1. Scope

This policy applies to data processed through JobsHunter web applications, internal APIs, and AI-assisted integrations that execute authorized actions on behalf of authenticated users.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account and profile data: username, email, phone number, location, preferences, job profile settings.
  • Authentication data: OAuth identity claims, session metadata, security-related logs.
  • Usage data: requests sent to JobsHunter tools, selected providers/models, timestamps, and technical diagnostics.
  • User content: uploaded CV metadata, prompts/preferences used for job search personalization.

3. Why We Process Data

  • To authenticate users and secure access to protected endpoints.
  • To execute job search requests and return relevant results.
  • To personalize search behavior based on user profile and prompt preferences.
  • To monitor reliability, investigate incidents, and prevent abuse.

4. Legal Basis and Consent

JobsHunter processes personal data for service delivery, security obligations, and legitimate operational interests. Where required by law, JobsHunter relies on user consent and allows users to withdraw consent for optional processing.

5. Sharing and Processors

JobsHunter may share limited data with infrastructure and identity providers strictly to deliver the service (for example, OAuth providers, hosting, and monitoring components). JobsHunter does not sell personal data.

6. Data Retention

JobsHunter keeps data only for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, and maintain security/audit trails. Retention periods may differ by data category and operational requirements.

7. Security Measures

JobsHunter uses layered controls such as authenticated access, transport security, request validation, and operational monitoring. No internet-based system is fully risk-free, but JobsHunter continuously improves defenses.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete personal data. You may also object to certain processing activities and request account/data removal.

9. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact JobsHunter support through the official support channels available in the JobsHunter product interface.

10. Policy Updates

JobsHunter may update this policy when legal, operational, or technical changes require it. The most recent version is always published on this page.
